01 / The Business Model

A 3-Stage Capital & Scale Engine

We do not pitch an endless capital burn. We use a disciplined 3-stage model: proving unit economics on a hero product, establishing shared lasting tooling, and scaling a global royalty-share network for creators.

Phase 1 • Immediate

Flagship Proof of Concept

Produce and distribute the 200-pair hero brushed suede low-top ($280 MSRP = $56,000 gross). Proves factory QA, establishes our supply chain, and validates global consumer appetite for artisan-textured footwear.

~58% Gross Margin on Direct-to-Consumer Sales
Phase 2 • Scale

The Designer Foundry

Reinvest profits to amortize our shared-sole molds across outside collaborators. Independent designers from Tokyo to London submit concepts; we fund the 200-pair runs, distribute globally, and split revenues via transparent royalties.

20–25% Platform Revenue-Share Per Creator Drop
Phase 3 • Network

Provenance & Global Distribution

Monetize physical/digital serial authentication (The "First 200" ledger) and broker breakout designer styles into high-end global boutique retail (comparable to the DSW × JEMS model), taking brokerage fees.

15–20% Wholesale Brokerage & Licensing Take-Rate

Δ A Necessary Reframe on Sneaker Resale

The speculative hype sneaker market has cooled substantially. We do not sell shoes as financial instruments. The downside of buying a VELD pair is small: the customer owns a timeless, wearable, handcrafted object that lasts for years.

The upside is cultural: owning a numbered first drop from an independent designer who may become a dominant name in modern fashion.
